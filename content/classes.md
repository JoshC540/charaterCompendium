# Melee

## Soldier
hp: 15
hit die: 1d12
defence: +1
weapons: Swords, spears, polearms, shields
trait: Hold the Line

The soldier is your basic melee class.

### Hold the Line
Gives +2 to melee and dex saves to the soldier and the companions next to them, if they are standing in a battle line, capped at +4.

## Berserker
hp: 15
hit die: 1d12
defence: +2
weapons: Swords, axes
trait: Blood Rage

The Berserker is high damage and strives to kill as many foes as possible before being cut down themselves.

### Blood Rage
Once the berserker is below 50% hp all damage out is doubled and all damage received is halved. Unable to dodge while berserk.

## Gladiator
hp: 12
hit die: 1d10
defence: +1
weapons: Any melee weapon, nets
trait: Pit Fighter

A gladiator is trained to be capable of using an assortment of weapons in order to please a crowd.

### Pit Fighter
The gladiator has +2 melee against any humanoid foe.

## Wardancer
hp: 12
hit die: 1d10
weapons: Dual swords, glaives
trait: Flow of the Battle

Wardancers in battle appear to flow between foes cutting them down.

### Flow of the Battle
Allows the wardancer to take an additional attack action each turn.

## Duelist
hp: 12
hit die: 1d10
weapons: Polearms, swords
trait: One on One Combat

Duelling is a practice as old as time itself and the duelists aim to master this art.

### One on One Combat
The duelist will never be engaged by more than one foe at a given time. Their opponent can be buffed by allies during the duel.

## Paladin
hp: 25
hit die: 1d20
defence: +2
weapons: Tower shields, swords, warhammers
trait: Protector

Paladins are made to hold a line until the bitter end. They are the core of a shield wall and have used them to great success in the past; they also excel at protecting weaker teammates.

### Protector
Allows the paladin to select a teammate and pull all enemy attacks towards that ally to them. A wisdom check is required to maintain this ability each turn, and the paladin is unable to dodge while using it.

## Monk
hp: 20
hit die: 1d20
defence: +2
weapons: Staffs, barehanded
trait: Divine Protection

Monks are said to have spent years studying both the art of combat and the teachings of their divinity.

### Divine Protection
Once per combat, should the monk's hp drop to 0, they will be able to continue to act for one additional turn before entering death saves. If healed during this time they will not die at the end of the effect.

## Rogue
hp: 10
hit die: 1d8
weapons: Knives, daggers, short swords
trait: From the Shadows

Rogues excel at taking out a target with the element of surprise; due to their lower hp pool it is the only style of combat which they can efficiently utilise.

### From the Shadows
At the end of their turns they roll a d4 and on a 4 they fade into the shadows, hiding from enemies during their turn. 1d2 to go into the shadows out of combat. When out of vision or not being looked for, you do not need to roll to enter the shadows out of combat.

## Reaper
hp: 10
hit die: 1d6
weapons: Curve-bladed weapons — sickles, scythes, kama, curved swords, hook swords
trait: Ethereal Form

### Ethereal Form
For 5 turns, all physical damage is halved, your attacks bypass armour, and you are able to traverse through narrow walls and floors.

## Blade Singer
hp: 15
hit die: 1d8
weapons: Swords, knives, daggers (limited to carrying 3 weapons)
trait: Blade Song

Blade singers are connected with their weapons at a spiritual level, allowing their blades to act of their own accord.

### Blade Song
Any weapon they don't attack with on their turn may perform one of the following two actions:
- **Attack** – attacks a foe for half damage
- **Defend** – grants advantage on dodge against the next attack, or it will attempt to destroy the next arrow shot at you

## Cavalier
hp: 15
hit die: 1d12
defence: +1
weapons: Lances, spears, swords, shields
trait: Bonded Mount

Cavaliers are mounted warriors who fight as one with their steed.

### Bonded Mount
Grants a mount with its own stat block that shares their initiative and levels alongside them, gaining HP and a stat boost whenever the cavalier does. Should the mount fall, a new one may be bonded over a long rest.

# Ranged

## Ranger
hp: 10
hit die: 1d6
weapons: Bows; short axes as a secondary
trait: Hail of Arrows

Rangers are highly trained archers who can fire faster than any other archer.

### Hail of Arrows
Allows the ranger to shoot 1d3 times a turn, dealing 50% damage.

## Hunter
hp: 15
hit die: 1d12
weapons: Bows, crossbows; short axes and daggers as a secondary
trait: Hunter's Instinct

Hunters spend years in the forest hunting creatures of all shapes and sizes, making use of all sorts of traps.

### Hunter's Instinct
Allows the hunter to spot the leader of a group in an instant. Gives the hunter +2 on all dex rolls if engaged by the leader and +2 on ranged rolls against them.

## Wildsman
hp: 20
hit die: 1d12
weapons: Bow, knives
trait: Guardian of Nature

Wildsmen live their lives in the forests and woods, helping to maintain a balance in nature and protecting it from external threats. They always have a companion creature with them for company.

### Guardian of Nature
Grants them a companion creature and prevents them from being attacked by wild animals unless they provoke the animal or the animal is under the effects of magic.

## Waywatcher
hp: 10
hit die: 1d6
weapons: Long bows, short bows
trait: Watcher

Waywatchers are often tasked with single-handedly observing a location or pathway. They have trained themselves to be masters of the bow.

### Watcher
Grants them perfect camouflage if they remain still for 1 hour, or 2 turns in combat. They also gain the effects of a long rest from a short rest and allow a party to bypass the long rest requirements.

## Trapper
hp: 15
hit die: 1d12
weapons: Crossbows; knives, axes or a spear as a secondary
trait: Right Trap for the Job

Trappers often work in tandem with hunters and trackers. They carry all sorts of traps, both lethal and non-lethal.

### Right Trap for the Job
Allows the trapper to construct a trap to catch any single foe or group of foes (up to 3 per trap, except bosses) if lured into it.

## Marksman
hp: 8
hit die: 1d4
weapons: Longbows; short bow for close quarters
trait: Hawk Eye

Marksmen don't miss what they shoot at. They also happen to only use bows, even in close quarters combat.

### Hawk Eye
Unless a ranged roll is a nat 1, they will hit the target on a failed roll for half damage.

## Harrier
hp: 12
hit die: 1d8
weapons: Bolas, nets, weighted lines; short bows, daggers or a short sword as a secondary
trait: Tangle

Harriers bring their quarry to heel with thrown restraints rather than killing blows.

### Tangle
Their bolas, nets and weighted lines entangle, trip or drag a target. A tangled foe is restrained and must pass a STR save to break free, with the difficulty scaling off the harrier's dexterity.

# Magic

## Mage
hp: 10
hit die: 1d6
weapons: Usually only a knife
trait: Detect Magic
magic: Elemental (Fire, Water, Earth, Air)

Mages are practitioners of the magics, and can wield them to great effect in combat to destroy hordes or single targets alike. Mages disdain martial combat.

### Detect Magic
Allows them to detect all the magic around them.

## Cleric
hp: 10
hit die: 1d6
weapons: Staffs
trait: Devout Follower
magic: Dawn Magic or Dusk Magic

Clerics are the counterparts of the monks; they instead focus on healing and spreading the word of their god. Clerics have been known to use staffs to focus their magic and, if needed, strike opponents.

### Devout Follower
The cleric heals for the value of the healing spell level used.

## Warlock
hp: 10
hit die: 1d6
weapons: Daggers, knives
trait: Otherworldly Power
magic: Warlock Magic / Angelic Magic

Warlocks are mages who, for one reason or another, sought power through more dangerous methods. Warlocks trade their souls to entities in exchange for great power.

### Otherworldly Power
Allows them to channel the entity they dealt with and summon a powerful spell of their chosen magic type.

## Seer
hp: 10
hit die: 1d6
weapons: Club, staff
trait: Foresight
magic: Augury Magic

Seers spend their time trying to predict what is to come.

### Foresight
Allows the seer to roll 3 d20 at the start of the session and assign each value rolled to one of the d20s rolled during the session.

## Enhancer
hp: 10
hit die: 1d6
weapons: Swords, short spears/javelins
trait: Soul Pull
magic: Enhancement Magic

Enhancers are known for their abilities to augment their allies, bolstering their strengths and mitigating their weaknesses.

### Soul Pull
Allows the enhancer to pull a target's soul to an alternate plane, one which is inhabited by demons. All damage taken here feels real but has no lasting physical effects, unless the target is a mage. The enhancer will not be attacked by demons here unless they remain there for too long. While in this alternate plane both the target's and the caster's physical bodies are unconscious.

## Druid
hp: 15
hit die: 1d10
weapons: Swords, glaives
trait: Detect Good and Evil
magic: Nature Magic

Druids cannot be taught their magics — you are either born a druid or you're not; there are no known exceptions to this rule. Druids utilise the forces of nature to give themselves an edge in combat.

### Detect Good and Evil
Allows the druid to sense the alignment of both items and people. This sense is based on your perception of good and evil.

## Shaman
hp: 12
hit die: 1d8
weapons: Axes, daggers, spears
trait: Ritual Cast
magic: Elemental, Nature and Shaman Magic

Shamans are magic wielders with the most natural teachings, generally being raised in a tribe and trained by the tribe's shaman to serve the tribe in the future. A shaman has access to their choice of spells from elemental and nature magic.

### Ritual Cast
Allows the Shaman to cast a spell without using a spell slot, taking 15 mins per tier of the spell. This cast is cancelled if the shaman ends early or is attacked.

## Hemomancer
hp: 15
hit die: 1d8
weapons: Ritual daggers, knives
trait: Blood Price
magic: Blood Magic (Rites)

Hemomancers wield their own life as a reagent, paying for power in blood rather than memorised spell slots. Their Rites cost HP by tier — 2 for tier 1, 4 for tier 2, 6 for tier 3 — with no daily limit beyond the blood left in their veins.

### Blood Price
Lets them overpay any Rite, spending double its HP cost to double its effect. Spent blood returns over rests and can never carry them above their maximum HP.

## Summoner
hp: 10
hit die: 1d6
weapons: Staff, daggers
trait: Bound Host
magic: Summoning Magic

Battlefield control and tactical presence. Summoners call entities to fight for them, with the kind of summoning expanded by their subclass.

### Bound Host
- You may have 2 active summons at a time.
- Summons act immediately after your turn, and issuing commands requires no action.
- If you are stunned or unconscious, summons defend you but do not pursue.
- Summons vanish at the end of combat unless your subclass says otherwise.
- When a summon dies, you take 1d4 backlash damage.

### Core Summoner Rules
- Summons can't dodge unless stated.
- AoE effects hit summons normally.
- Summons are considered magical entities.
- You may dismiss a summon freely on your turn.

# Other

## Tinkerer
hp: 8
hit die: 1d4
weapons: Hammers, axes and other tools
trait: Creation

The tinkerer displays an affinity with all things mechanical, allowing them to dream up and create specialised tools and equipment to complete a task.

### Creation
Allows the tinkerer to create a tool or piece of equipment with available material once per session.

## Beastmaster
hp: 12
hit die: 1d10
weapons: Their tames; spears, javelins, axes, shields
trait: Beast Whispering

Beastmasters tame and befriend creatures whilst also caring for a party's existing tames/familiars.

### Beast Whispering
You may tame beasts within your wildness ability. Beasts given orders will act accordingly based on order and wildness. A beast's wildness stat will increase and decrease based on your actions. If you have a wildness stat past what you can manage, any 5+ wildness beasts are likely to turn on you.

### Wildness
- **5+:** Totally wild/feral. Is following you out of personal benefit, is not loyal, and if needed/in its best interests it may turn on you or the party.
- **4:** Slightly domesticated. Will hear orders, may act on them if they do not conflict with the creature's personal safety or beliefs.
- **3:** Domesticated. Will hear orders, will act on them if they do not conflict with the creature's personal safety or beliefs.
- **2:** Friendly. Will hear orders, will act on them if they do not conflict with the creature's beliefs. Will attempt to keep the party safe even when not instructed but will not risk personal harm unless ordered.
- **1:** Very friendly. Will hear orders, will act on them if they do not conflict with the creature's beliefs. Will attempt to keep the party safe even when not instructed.
- **0:** Totally tamed. Will hear orders, will obey them without hesitation, will die for the party.

### Tame healing
Most tames heal as follows: small creatures 1d4 each rest, medium creatures 1d8, large creatures 1d12 and huge creatures 1d20.

## Tracker
hp: 12
hit die: 1d10
weapons: Swords, spears, axes, occasionally daggers
trait: Thrill of the Hunt

A skilled tracker is said to rival the tracking skills of a bloodhound, and is usually less aggressive.

### Thrill of the Hunt
Gives the Tracker +3 to perception while following a trail and makes the party immune to ambushes whilst hunting a target.

## Siege Master
hp: 8
hit die: 1d4
weapons: Slings, crossbows, hammers, axes, knives — and their siege engines
trait: Siege Time

If you need to destroy a large structure the siege master is the one for the job. The siege master travels with the tools to create siege engines.

### Siege Time
Allows the siege master to create one of the following: ballista, catapult, trebuchet, battering ram, scaling ladder or siege tower. They take 3 turns to make and there can only be one out at a time.

## Bard
hp: 8
hit die: 1d4
weapons: Knives and daggers, if forced into single combat
trait: Inspire

Bards are storytellers, telling tales of what they claim transpired during their travels. Often there are nuggets of wisdom to be found within their tales. They also have access to some pseudo-magic called performances.

### Inspire
Spend an inspiration point to inspire a target, allowing them to add a 1d6 to a single roll (checks only). You have 3 inspiration points per day.

## Trickster
hp: 12
hit die: 1d6
weapons: A standard deck of cards
trait: Luck of the Draw

A trickster puts their fate in the hands of luck and often comes out on top. They are often found in gambling dens winning far more than they have any right to, and should they be attacked after their winnings, they make the cards themselves into a deadly weapon.

### Luck of the Draw — out of combat
The suits have the following effects on targets:
- **Hearts** – 10 minute charm
- **Clubs** – 10 minute berserk
- **Spades** – forget what just happened
- **Diamonds** – 1 favourable deal

### Luck of the Draw — in combat
Cards deal damage equal to the value on the card (face cards are worth 15 and aces 20). Jacks trigger the minor effect, queens the effect and kings the greater effect. Aces trigger the effect and a random effect.
- **Hearts – Bleed:** minor 1 stack, base 5 stacks, greater 10 stacks
- **Diamonds – Temp HP:** minor 5, base 10, greater 12 + 1d20
- **Clubs – Stun:** minor 1 turn, base 1d3 turns, greater 1d4 + 2 turns
- **Spades – Bonus damage:** minor +5, base +6 + 1d6, greater +8 + 1d10

## Runesmith
hp: 12
hit die: 1d8
weapons: Warhammers, axes, rune-etched daggers
trait: Inscribe

Runesmiths do not cast in the heat of the moment; their power is laid down ahead of time and waits to be unleashed.

### Inscribe
Lets them carve runes onto gear, allies, the ground or objects during a rest, each lying dormant until its trigger — a spoken word, a touch, an enemy crossing it — sets it off.

## Skinwalker
hp: 12
hit die: 1d10
weapons: Daggers, claws, bone weapons
trait: Wear the Skin

Skinwalkers claim the skins of the creatures they slay and wear them as their own.

### Wear the Skin
Lets them take the full form of any creature whose skin they have claimed. In a worn form they gain that creature's stat block, movement and natural attacks while keeping their own HP, traits and subclass abilities. Changing form is a bonus action.

## Pirate
hp: 12
hit die: 1d8
weapons: Cutlasses, knives, hand crossbows
trait: Swashbuckler's Luck

Pirates throw themselves into danger and trust fortune to drag them back out.

### Swashbuckler's Luck
Reroll an attack, dodge or daring stunt and take the better result, three times a day — five when at sea. If the new roll lands a natural 20 they earn a moment of legend: disarming a foe, rallying allies or escaping certain doom in style (rule of cool, doubled).
