# Introduction

Levelling, Soft Skills, Feats & Traits

| *This document covers everything your character gains as they level: what each level gives you, and the full lists of soft skills, feats and traits. It replaces the old Leveling System, Feats, and Soft Skills/Tools/Instruments documents, and the Traits/Proficiencies part of the point shop.* |

# Quick start

When your character levels up:

1. Look at the last digit of your new level and find it in the **Each decade** table (level 23 is X3, level 40 is X10). Take what it gives.
2. If you gained a soft skill rank, put it into a skill from **Soft skills**. If you gained a feat, choose one from **Feats** at a tier your Renown allows.
3. On a level ending in 0, your Renown goes up by 1. Pick a milestone boon, then check which feats and skills you can now reach.

Traits work differently: you choose Drawbacks and Quirks when you create your character, and the DM awards Marks during play.

# Core rules

## Bonuses and penalties

Stats are roll-under: a lower number is better (5 is strong, 14 is weak).

  - **+X to a roll** makes that one roll X easier. **−X** makes it X harder. The stat itself doesn't change.
  - **Improve a stat by 1** means its number goes down by 1, permanently. **Worsen a stat by 1** means it goes up by 1.
  - **Advantage** and **disadvantage** work as in the core rules.

## Renown

Renown is your standing as a hero. Many feats and skills get stronger as it rises.

| Renown = 1 + your level ÷ 10, rounded down. |

| Level | Renown | Title |
| **0–9** | 1 | Starting adventurer |
| **10–19** | 2 | Local hero |
| **20–29** | 3 | District hero |
| **30–49** | 4–5 | National hero |
| **50–69** | 6–7 | International hero |
| **70–99** | 8–10 | Dimensional hero |
| **100+** | 11+ | Interdimensional hero |

When an effect says "+Renown" or "2×Renown", use your current Renown. A level 34 character has Renown 4, so "2×Renown damage" is 8.

## Stats and terms

  - **Stats:** STR, DEX, INT, WIS, PER, CHA.
  - **Combat stats:** Melee, Ranged, Dodge, Disengage.
  - **Save:** a stat roll you make to resist something (a "WIS save" is a WIS roll to resist).
  - **Reaction:** one action per round taken outside your own turn.
  - **Per rest:** recharges after a short or long rest. **Per long rest**, **per day**, **per session** and **per arc** mean exactly that.
  - **Arc:** one story arc of the campaign, as the DM decides.

# Levelling

Levelling runs in levels of ten. Levels 1–10 set the pattern, and it repeats for 11–20, 21–30 and so on: level 13 gives the same as level 3, level 48 the same as level 8. Level 0 is your base class, starting adventurer, Renown 1.

## Each decade

| Level | You gain |
| **X1** | 1 SP. Improve any one stat by 1, but not below 10. |
| **X2** | Roll your class hit die and add it to your max HP. |
| **X3** | 1 SP. |
| **X4** | Improve any one stat by 1, but not below 5. Gain 1 soft skill rank. |
| **X5** | 1 SP. |
| **X6** | Gain 1 soft skill rank. |
| **X7** | 1 SP. |
| **X8** | Gain 1 feat of Tier I, II or III that you qualify for. |
| **X9** | 1 SP. |
| **X10** | 1 SP. Roll your class hit die for max HP. Your Renown goes up by 1 and you take a milestone boon. |

X1's stat improvement can only lift a weak stat as far as 10; X4's can push any stat as far as 5.

Every 10 levels adds up to 6 SP, 2 stat improvements, 2 hit dice, 2 soft skill ranks, 1 feat and 1 milestone boon.

From level 11 onwards, you may take FP instead of SP on any SP level, if you can spend it.

## Milestone boons

At every level 10, 20, 30 and so on, choose one:

  - Improve one combat stat (Melee, Ranged, Dodge or Disengage) by 1, but not below 5.
  - Gain 2 soft skill ranks.
  - Retrain: swap one feat for another you qualify for, and move up to 2 soft skill ranks to different skills.
  - **Renown 6 or higher:** gain one soft skill rank past Master (Grandmaster or Legendary, if you meet its Renown).
  - **Renown 8 or higher:** take one Tier IV feat you qualify for, or improve two combat stats by 1 (not below 5).
  - **Renown 11 or higher:** take one Tier V feat you qualify for, or claim a Mythic Mark for a deed your character has already done (DM approval).

You also take the new hero title (Local, District, National and so on), and the DM may give it one consequence in the world: a patron, a bounty, a rival, a door that now opens.

# Soft skills

Soft skills are the things your character can do outside a fight: crafts, trades, lore, survival know-how. There are 56, in six families. Each skill includes its tools, so being trained in Smithing means you can use a forge and smith's tools. Instruments are part of Performance, and languages are part of Linguistics.

You put ranks into a skill. You gain ranks at levels X4 and X6, and from milestone boons.

## Ranks

| Rank | Needs | What you can do |
| **Trained** | 1 rank | +2 to rolls using the skill. You can do the work to a normal standard and use its tools. |
| **Expert** | 2 ranks | +4 instead. Work takes half the time and can be fine quality (worth double). You can teach the skill. |
| **Master** | 3 ranks, Renown 2 | +6 instead. Routine tasks succeed without a roll. You gain a Master signature. |
| **Grandmaster** | 4 ranks, Renown 6 | +8 instead. You can attempt the impossible within the skill: the DM sets a quest-level cost (a rare material, a teacher, a journey) instead of a roll. |
| **Legendary** | 5 ranks, Renown 9 | +10 instead. Once per session, the skill does something no mortal could. |

You can only be a Master in one skill per family until Renown 5, and you can only hold one Legendary skill until Renown 11.

## Family bonuses

Hold Expert or better in three skills of the same family and you gain that family's bonus.

| Family | Family bonus |
| **Crafting** | Make masterwork items by default: pick one of +1 damage, +1 defence, or one minor property to all crafts.  |
| **Arts & Letters** | Once per session, name a contact, rumour or text you'd plausibly know; the DM tells you something true about it. |
| **Nature** | Your party can't get lost, go hungry or be caught out by terrain hazards in the wild. |
| **Trade & Underworld** | Once per session, one deal, bribe or buyer goes your way without a roll. |
| **Body & Wits** | Once per session, turn a failed physical roll (not an attack) into a success. |
| **Mythic Lore** | You're a recognised authority beyond the mortal world. Once per arc, an outsider seeks you out with something it wants you to know. |

## How each skill scales

Every skill below lists what it adds at each rank. Ranks stack: an Expert keeps everything from Trained, adds the Expert line, and swaps +2 for +4 from the Ranks table above. A Master has all three lines, and so on up.

The rank requirements still apply: Master needs Renown 2, Grandmaster Renown 6 and Legendary Renown 9. A Legendary ability can be used once per session unless it says otherwise, and the DM approves exactly how it plays out.

## Crafting

### Smithing

*Covers: blacksmithing, forge, smith's tools*

  - **Trained:** Repair and sharpen metal weapons and armour; forge tools, nails and simple blades.
  - **Expert:** Forge any standard weapon or metal armour; fine-quality weapons you make deal +1 damage.
  - **Master:** Reforge a weapon so its damage die goes up one size (d8 to d10), once per item.
  - **Grandmaster:** Work starmetal, dragonbone and other exotic metals; a weapon you forge can carry one major property.
  - **Legendary:** Forge a weapon that breaks a rule: it cuts spells, wounds the incorporeal, or never misses its sworn foe.

### Woodworking

*Covers: carpentry, boatmaking, shipwright's tools*

  - **Trained:** Build and repair furniture, doors, simple shelters and wooden gear.
  - **Expert:** Build carts, rowing boats and barricades; allies behind a barricade you raise get +2 to Dodge.
  - **Master:** With a crew of 3, build a small boat, cart or cabin in a single day.
  - **Grandmaster:** Work ironwood and living wood; build a seagoing ship, keep or bridge in a tenth of the usual time.
  - **Legendary:** Raise a wooden fort, ship or great hall overnight from the forest around you.

### Fletching & Bowyery

*Covers: fletching, bowmaking, fletcher's and bowyer's tools*

  - **Trained:** Make and repair arrows, bolts and simple bows.
  - **Expert:** Craft longbows and crossbows; fine arrows give +1 to Ranged rolls.
  - **Master:** Each rest, craft 5 trick arrows: barbed (+1d4), whistling (signal) or rope (anchor line), etc.
  - **Grandmaster:** Bows you craft add 2xRenown damage to every shot; trick arrows can carry a stored tier one spell.
  - **Legendary:** Craft a bow that fires arrows of light and never runs out of ammunition.

### Leatherworking

*Covers: leatherworking, cobbling, furrier's and taxidermist's tools*

  - **Trained:** Make and repair leather armour, boots, packs and saddles.
  - **Expert:** Skin and cure monster hides without spoiling them; fine leather gear weighs nothing for carrying.
  - **Master:** Leather armour you make gets +1 defence and never hampers stealth.
  - **Grandmaster:** Armour made from a monster's hide gives one of its resistances (fire from a salamander, poison from a wyvern).
  - **Legendary:** Craft armour or a cloak from a legendary beast's hide that lets the wearer use one of the beast's powers.

### Textiles

*Covers: tailoring, weaving, basket weaving, rope and net making, clothier's and haberdasher's tools*

  - **Trained:** Sew, mend and weave clothing, rope, nets and baskets.
  - **Expert:** Make fine clothing (+2 to CHA rolls in high society while worn) and convincing costumes.
  - **Master:** Sewn-in pockets can't be found by a normal search; your rope never snaps by accident.
  - **Grandmaster:** Weave cloth that holds a minor enchantment: it warms, cools, sheds water or shrugs off flame.
  - **Legendary:** Weave something impossible: a rope that climbs on command, a cloak of living shadow, a sail that needs no wind.

### Jewelcraft

*Covers: jewelry crafting, gemcutter's tools*

  - **Trained:** Cut common gems, make simple rings and settings, and tell real gems from glass.
  - **Expert:** Fine jewellery sells for double and gives +2 to CHA rolls with nobility while worn.
  - **Master:** Set a gem to store one tier one spell, released once by whoever wears it.
  - **Grandmaster:** Stored spells can be up to tier two, and they recharge after each long rest.
  - **Legendary:** Make a crown, ring or amulet that holds a sliver of a god's, dragon's or plane's power.

### Glass & Pottery

*Covers: glassblowing, pottery, potter's tools*

  - **Trained:** Make bottles, jars, bowls and simple glassware.
  - **Expert:** Make alchemist's glassware and sealed jars that keep their contents fresh for a year.
  - **Master:** Grind lenses (a spyglass gives +2 to long-range PER) or make vials that never break.
  - **Grandmaster:** Make a scrying glass: once per long rest, watch a place you know for one minute.
  - **Legendary:** Make a mirror or vessel that traps a spirit, holds a storm, or shows only the truth.

### Stonework

*Covers: masonry, sculpting, plastering, architecture, mining, gravedigging*

  - **Trained:** Cut and lay stone, dig safely, and judge whether a structure is sound.
  - **Expert:** Raise walls in half the time; advantage on spotting traps built into stonework.
  - **Master:** Spot weak points: +2×Renown damage to structures, and you always find hidden doors in stonework.
  - **Grandmaster:** Carve stone that holds a ward or glyph; with tools, tunnel through solid rock at walking pace.
  - **Legendary:** Bring down a fortress with one strike at its keystone, or raise a stone tower from bedrock in a day.

### Alchemy

*Covers: alchemy, candle making, chandler's tools, perfumer's kit*

  - **Trained:** Brew basic remedies, candles, soaps, perfumes and acids.
  - **Expert:** Brew healing potions and alchemical bombs (1d6 + Renown damage).
  - **Master:** Brew potions at half cost; your healing potions heal one extra die.
  - **Grandmaster:** Brew potions that copy the effect of a tier two spell.
  - **Legendary:** Brew an elixir that does the impossible: regrows a lost limb, adds a decade of life, or turns lead to gold.

### Enchanting

*Covers: enchanter's tools, runes, gem dust, essences*

  - **Trained:** Read and identify any enchantment by touch, and recharge simple charged items during a rest.
  - **Expert:** Place minor enchantments on items (a blade that never dulls, a lantern that never burns out, boots that leave no tracks); safely strip an enchantment from an item and keep its essence for reuse.
  - **Master:** Bind two minor enchantments to the same item, and enchantments you make can't be dispelled by tier five or less magic.
  - **Grandmaster:** Enchant with exotic essences (dragon, void, celestial) so an item can carry one major property.
  - **Legendary:** Enchant something that isn't an item: a place, a person's shadow, a single moment.

### Poisoncraft

*Covers: poisoner's kit, venom harvesting*

  - **Trained:** Identify common poisons and harvest venom safely.
  - **Expert:** Brew and apply poisons (1d6 + Renown poison damage, or a save-or-suffer effect) and their antidotes.
  - **Master:** Targets of your poisons take −2 on their saves against them.
  - **Grandmaster:** Brew poisons that work on creatures normally immune, or that take effect days later.
  - **Legendary:** Brew a poison no magic can detect, or one that can kill a god.

### Brewing

*Covers: brewing, vintner's tools*

  - **Trained:** Brew ale, mead and simple wines.
  - **Expert:** Fine brews sell for double; a strong brew gives allies +2 to saves against fear for an hour.
  - **Master:** A signature brew: +2 to CHA rolls with anyone you share it with, and it sells for double.
  - **Grandmaster:** Brew drinks with magical effects: courage, truth-telling, or forgetting the last hour.
  - **Legendary:** Brew a drink famous across worlds: one sip shows the drinker their truest desire or mends a broken mind.

### Cooking

*Covers: cooking, baking, butchery*

  - **Trained:** Cook decent meals from almost anything, and preserve food for travel.
  - **Expert:** Feed the party on half rations; a hot meal ends one minor ailment.
  - **Master:** A meal you cook on a long rest gives the party 2×Renown temporary HP.
  - **Grandmaster:** A feast you cook gives every diner +2 to one stat roll of their choice the next day.
  - **Legendary:** Cook a feast that heals every wound at the table, or one that stops everyone who shares it from harming each other until dawn.

### Engineering

*Covers: siege engines, bridges, pulleys, mechanisms*

  - **Trained:** Read plans and build simple bridges, scaffolds and lifting rigs.
  - **Expert:** Crew and repair siege engines; advantage on finding a structure's weak point.
  - **Master:** Build bridges in half the time; engines you crew get +1 use.
  - **Grandmaster:** Design siege engines that deal +2×Renown damage and can be broken down and carried.
  - **Legendary:** Build a wonder: a moving fortress, a sky bridge, a defence that covers a whole city (one arc to build).

## Arts & Letters

### Scribing

*Covers: scribing, bookbinding, paper and parchment making, scribe's and librarian's kits*

  - **Trained:** Read and write a clean hand, bind books and copy documents.
  - **Expert:** Copy documents in half the time, and find what you need in any library within an hour.
  - **Master:** Copy any text perfectly, including spell scrolls you can read.
  - **Grandmaster:** Write spell scrolls of spells you know, usable by anyone who can read them.
  - **Legendary:** Write a book that changes its reader: one chapter teaches a soft skill rank or a tier one spell (once per reader).

### Fine Arts

*Covers: painting, tattooing, scrimshaw*

  - **Trained:** Paint, draw and tattoo well; sketch a face well enough for others to recognise it.
  - **Expert:** Your work sells for double, and sketches you make from memory are exact.
  - **Master:** A portrait or tattoo you make is a keepsake: once per session its owner gets +2 on one roll.
  - **Grandmaster:** Tattoos you ink can hold a minor enchantment: a resistance, sight in the dark, or a ward.
  - **Legendary:** Paint something real: a door that opens, a beast that steps out for one scene, a portrait that speaks.

### Performance

*Covers: instruments, singing, storytelling*

  - **Trained:** Play 2 instruments, sing and tell stories well enough to earn a living.
  - **Expert:** Learn 2 more instruments; a performance can calm a crowd or start a brawl.
  - **Master:** Once per rest, perform: every ally who hears gets +1d4 on their next roll.
  - **Grandmaster:** Once per long rest, a one-minute performance ends fear and charm on allies who hear it and gives them 2×Renown temporary HP.
  - **Legendary:** A performance that moves the world: it stops a battle, sways a court, or makes a god pause to listen.

### Linguistics

*Covers: languages, scripts, codes*

  - **Trained:** Learn 1 language; read and copy unfamiliar scripts.
  - **Expert:** Learn 1 more language; crack simple codes and ciphers in an hour.
  - **Master:** Understand the gist of any spoken language after a minute of listening.
  - **Grandmaster:** Read any written language, living or dead, given an hour.
  - **Legendary:** Any creature that understands language understands you, including beasts and the dead.

### Astrology

*Covers: astrologer's tools, star lore, omens*

  - **Trained:** Read the stars for the date, season and direction.
  - **Expert:** Once per long rest, read the omens: the DM says whether today favours a plan (good, bad or mixed).
  - **Master:** Once per session, read the stars and ask the DM one yes/no question about a coming event.
  - **Grandmaster:** Ask two questions per session, and they can be open questions instead of yes/no.
  - **Legendary:** Read a fate in full: the DM tells you how a named person's story, npc only,  is set to end (If there is a clear way it will end).

### History & Lore

*Covers: ruins, fallen kingdoms, legends*

  - **Trained:** Know the great kingdoms, wars and legends of the land.
  - **Expert:** Date any object or building by sight; +2 more to rolls about ruins and relics.
  - **Master:** Identify any historic site, relic or legend, and learn one weakness of a named legendary creature.
  - **Grandmaster:** With a week of research, find any lost place a legend mentions.
  - **Legendary:** The DM tells you one forgotten truth about the matter at hand, something no one alive knows.

### Heraldry & Etiquette

*Covers: noble houses, court manners, high society*

  - **Trained:** Recognise noble houses by their arms and follow basic court manners.
  - **Expert:** Read the politics of a room: who holds power, and who speaks for a hidden patron.
  - **Master:** You never commit a social blunder; +2 to CHA rolls with nobility; win an audience with a lord within a day.
  - **Grandmaster:** Once per arc, broker a marriage, alliance or treaty between houses.
  - **Legendary:** Your word settles a contested claim to a title or throne, and every court accepts it.

### Theology

*Covers: faiths, rites, gods and their servants*

  - **Trained:** Know the major faiths, their gods and holy days.
  - **Expert:** Lead services and rites; +2 to CHA rolls with the faithful.
  - **Master:** Perform any faith's rites; clergy of any religion treat you as an honoured guest.
  - **Grandmaster:** Once per arc, call on a temple of any faith for sanctuary, healing or soldiers.
  - **Legendary:** Perform a rite a god must witness: it sanctifies, curses, or ends a holy war.

### Forgery

*Covers: forgery kit, seals, signatures, documents*

  - **Trained:** Copy simple handwriting and spot crude fakes.
  - **Expert:** Forge travel papers, letters and seals that pass a casual look.
  - **Master:** Your forgeries pass any mundane inspection; only magic exposes them.
  - **Grandmaster:** Your forgeries fool divination and magical inspection too.
  - **Legendary:** Forge a document that becomes true: a royal decree, a deed or a contract that binds as if real.

## Nature

### Tracking

*Covers: tracking, reading sign*

  - **Trained:** Follow clear tracks and tell roughly how old they are.
  - **Expert:** Track over hard ground and through rain; tell if the quarry was wounded or heavily loaded.
  - **Master:** Follow a trail up to a week old and know the quarry's number, size and condition.
  - **Grandmaster:** Follow trails up to a season old, across water, and through teleportation or planar travel.
  - **Legendary:** Track anything that has passed through, even a ghost.

### Herbalism

*Covers: herbalism, foraging, gardening*

  - **Trained:** Find edible plants and common medicinal herbs.
  - **Expert:** Make poultices that heal 1d4 + Renown, and recognise every poisonous plant.
  - **Master:** Gather one healing herb each rest; it heals 1d6 + Renown.
  - **Grandmaster:** Find rare reagents for potions, enchanting or rituals in any natural region within a day.
  - **Legendary:** Find a plant out of legend: a flower that cures any disease, or a root that brings back someone dead less than a day.

### Hunting & Trapping

*Covers: hunting, skinning, snares*

  - **Trained:** Hunt small game and set snares that keep the party fed.
  - **Expert:** Hunt large game; traps you set deal 1d6 and take a STR save to escape.
  - **Master:** Traps you set deal +1d6 and their STR save is 2 harder to beat.
  - **Grandmaster:** Trap monsters: a trap you set can hold a Huge creature for a round.
  - **Legendary:** Trap anything: a spirit, a storm, or a creature from beyond the planes.

### Fishing

*Covers: fishing, fisher's tackle*

  - **Trained:** Catch a meal's worth of fish in most waters.
  - **Expert:** Feed the party for a day from any river, lake or coast.
  - **Master:** The party is always fed near water; once per session you land something unusual.
  - **Grandmaster:** Fish rare creatures or lost items up from any depth.
  - **Legendary:** Hook a legend: a sea beast, a drowned relic, or a fish that grants one wish.

### Animal Handling

*Covers: animal handling, husbandry, falconry, beekeeping, farrier's and herding tools*

  - **Trained:** Calm, feed and care for common animals.
  - **Expert:** Train an animal to obey simple commands within a week.
  - **Master:** Calm any non-magical beast without a roll; companions you train get +2 on one stat.
  - **Grandmaster:** Tame and train magical beasts (griffons, dire wolves) given a month.
  - **Legendary:** Command any beast that can hear you for one scene, magical or not.

### Riding

*Covers: everyday riding, mount care, tack*

  - **Trained:** Ride at speed without falling, and care for a mount.
  - **Expert:** Ride any common mount; your mount gets +10 ft movement.
  - **Master:** Your mount covers half again as much ground a day and never spooks.
  - **Grandmaster:** Ride flying and exotic mounts; your mount uses your Dodge.
  - **Legendary:** Ride anything.

### Agriculture

*Covers: farming, soil, blight, livestock feed*

  - **Trained:** Grow crops and tend livestock.
  - **Expert:** Double a farm's yield in a season; treat sick crops and animals.
  - **Master:** Judge land, crops and blight at a glance; a village you help owes you a favour.
  - **Grandmaster:** End a blight or famine across a district within a season.
  - **Legendary:** Make barren land bloom overnight: a desert, a battlefield or a cursed field.

### Navigation

*Covers: navigation, cartography, navigator's tools*

  - **Trained:** Read a map and find your way by sun and stars.
  - **Expert:** Draw accurate maps, and find the fastest route between two known places.
  - **Master:** You're never lost, and overland or sea journeys take 25% less time.
  - **Grandmaster:** Journeys take half the time, and you find ways through places thought impassable.
  - **Legendary:** Find any place, even one hidden by magic, lost to time, or on another plane.

### Swimming

*Covers: swimming, diving*

  - **Trained:** Swim in calm water and stay afloat for hours.
  - **Expert:** Swim in rough water and currents; hold your breath for 2 minutes.
  - **Master:** Swim at full speed and hold your breath for 10 minutes.
  - **Grandmaster:** Swim through storms and whirlpools without a roll; hold your breath for an hour.
  - **Legendary:** Swim through anything: up a waterfall, through lava (Will still take fire damage).

### Mountaineering

*Covers: climbing gear, ropes, altitude, ice*

  - **Trained:** Climb with ropes and gear, and pick safe routes up a slope.
  - **Expert:** Lead roped climbs; the party ignores mild altitude sickness.
  - **Master:** The party ignores altitude and cold hazards on mountains; roped climbs need no roll.
  - **Grandmaster:** Climb sheer ice and overhangs without gear; avalanches can't catch you.
  - **Legendary:** Climb anything: a glass tower, a living giant, the underside of a floating island.

### Weather Lore

*Covers: skies, winds, seasons*

  - **Trained:** Read the sky for the next few hours' weather.
  - **Expert:** Predict the weather a day ahead and find shelter before it turns.
  - **Master:** Predict weather 3 days ahead; the party is never caught unprepared by a storm.
  - **Grandmaster:** Once per day, nudge the weather: delay a storm by an hour or bring a light rain.
  - **Legendary:** Command the weather over a region for one scene: call lightning, part a storm, raise fog.

## Trade & Underworld

### Appraisal

*Covers: valuing goods, spotting fakes*

  - **Trained:** Know roughly what common goods are worth.
  - **Expert:** Value art and valuables to within 10%, and spot common fakes.
  - **Master:** Value any item exactly, and spot magical or fake goods by touch.
  - **Grandmaster:** Touch an item to learn its history: who made it and who owned it last.
  - **Legendary:** Know the true worth of anything, including a person's price.

### Haggling

*Covers: trading, bargaining, auctioneering*

  - **Trained:** Get fair prices and avoid being cheated.
  - **Expert:** Buy at 10% less and sell at 10% more.
  - **Master:** Buy at 25% less and sell at 25% more.
  - **Grandmaster:** Buy at half price and sell at 50% more; merchants start owing you favours.
  - **Legendary:** Strike a deal for something that isn't for sale.

### Logistics

*Covers: logistics, accounting, bookkeeping*

  - **Trained:** Keep accounts and plan supplies for a small party.
  - **Expert:** Supplies last a quarter longer; run a caravan's or warband's stores.
  - **Master:** Supplies last half again as long, and the party carries 50% more.
  - **Grandmaster:** Keep an army supplied in the field; supplies last twice as long.
  - **Legendary:** For one arc, the party's supplies never run out, however long the road.

### Sailing

*Covers: sailing, rigging, knots*

  - **Trained:** Crew a ship and handle small boats.
  - **Expert:** Captain a small ship and sail through bad weather without a roll.
  - **Master:** A ship needs half its usual crew under you, and you outrun ships your size.
  - **Grandmaster:** Sail through storms, reefs and fog that would sink others; your ship moves at double speed.
  - **Legendary:** Sail where no ship can: between planes, through the land of the dead.

### Smuggling & Locks

*Covers: smuggling, thieves' tools, locksmith's tools*

  - **Trained:** Pick simple locks and hide small items on a person or cart.
  - **Expert:** Pick good locks; compartments you build need a hard search to find.
  - **Master:** Open any mundane lock as one action; compartments you build can't be found without magic.
  - **Grandmaster:** Open magical locks and wards, and smuggle anything across any border.
  - **Legendary:** Open anything that has a way in.

### Gambling

*Covers: dice, cards, games of chance*

  - **Trained:** Know every common game and spot an obvious cheat.
  - **Expert:** Read other players' tells; +2 more to rolls in games of chance.
  - **Master:** Once per session, win a game you choose to win, cheating unnoticed.
  - **Grandmaster:** Play for stakes beyond money (secrets, favours, years of service), and the loser must pay.
  - **Legendary:** Bet on fate: name an outcome(Within reason) and roll against the DM; if you win, it happens.

### Disguise

*Covers: disguise kit, voices, mannerisms*

  - **Trained:** Change your look with costume and simple makeup.
  - **Expert:** Pass as a different trade, class or region, and change your voice convincingly.
  - **Master:** Your disguise holds under close inspection by anyone who doesn't know you well.
  - **Grandmaster:** Pass as a specific person, even to their friends, for one scene.
  - **Legendary:** Become someone else completely: magic, divination and their own family can't tell.

### Streetwise

*Covers: gangs, fences, rumour mills*

  - **Trained:** Find a cheap bed, a tavern rumour and how the local watch works.
  - **Expert:** Find black markets and gang territory, and learn who really runs a town.
  - **Master:** In any town, find a fence, a safehouse or a solid rumour within an hour.
  - **Grandmaster:** Run your own network across a region: once per arc, call in a job, a hideout or a favour.
  - **Legendary:** Any criminal outfit, anywhere, grants you one request.

## Body & Wits

### Medicine

*Covers: first aid, surgery, healing kits*

  - **Trained:** Clean and bind wounds and stop bleeding.
  - **Expert:** Set bones and treat disease and poison: your patient gets +2 to their save.
  - **Master:** Stabilise the dying without a roll; your patients regain an extra 2×Renown HP on a long rest.
  - **Grandmaster:** Perform surgery that cures lasting injuries, including Drawbacks from wounds (a lame leg, a lost eye).
  - **Legendary:** Within a thirty seconds of someone's death, bring them back to 1 HP.

### Acrobatics

*Covers: tumbling, balance, falling*

  - **Trained:** Tumble, keep your balance, and land safely from short drops.
  - **Expert:** Halve fall damage from up to 30 ft; +2 to Disengage rolls.
  - **Master:** Take half damage from falls, and once per turn move through an enemy's space.
  - **Grandmaster:** Take no fall damage; run along walls and across ropes at full speed.
  - **Legendary:** For one round, slip past every attack, through any gap, between the raindrops.

### Athletics

*Covers: running, jumping, lifting*

  - **Trained:** Run, jump and lift well beyond the untrained.
  - **Expert:** +5 ft movement; swim and climb at half speed without a roll.
  - **Master:** Jump twice as far and carry twice as much; +2 to grapple and shove rolls.
  - **Grandmaster:** Lift a loaded cart, hold a gate shut alone, or run all day without tiring.
  - **Legendary:** A feat of strength from legend: hold up a collapsing hall, or hurl a boulder across a river.

### Sleight of Hand

*Covers: pickpocketing, palming, tricks*

  - **Trained:** Palm coins, do card tricks and pick simple pockets.
  - **Expert:** Pick pockets in a crowd, and swap similar items unnoticed.
  - **Master:** Palm or plant a small item in plain view without a roll.
  - **Grandmaster:** Lift worn items (a ring, a key on a chain) or a sheathed weapon unnoticed.
  - **Legendary:** Steal something that can't be stolen: a voice, a name, a spell mid-cast.

### Endurance

*Covers: marching, fasting, heat and cold*

  - **Trained:** March a full day, and go a day without food, without penalty.
  - **Expert:** +2 to saves against exhaustion, heat and cold; sleep anywhere.
  - **Master:** Go 3 days without food or sleep before penalties; forced marches cost you nothing.
  - **Grandmaster:** Go a week without food, water or sleep; you always ignore the first level of exhaustion.
  - **Legendary:** For one scene, nothing stops you: not exhaustion, not wounds, not 0 HP. Once that scene ends, all things occur at once.

### Meditation

*Covers: focus, breathing, trance*

  - **Trained:** Calm your mind on command; +2 to saves against fear.
  - **Expert:** Sleep half as long, and as an action meditate to end being charmed or frightened.
  - **Master:** Once per day, a 10-minute trance counts as a short rest.
  - **Grandmaster:** Once per day, your trance counts as a long rest, and you stay aware of your surroundings in it.
  - **Legendary:** Step outside yourself: act freely for one scene while your body is held, possessed or unconscious.

## Mythic Lore

Nobody teaches these to a starting adventurer. You can't put a rank in one until you reach the Renown listed.

### Dragon Lore

*Needs Renown four. Covers: dragon kinds, Draconic, hoard law*

  - **Trained:** Recognise any dragon's kind and its breath.
  - **Expert:** Read hoard marks and draconic territory; +2 to CHA rolls with dragons.
  - **Master:** Speak Draconic, know any dragon's lineage on sight, and learn one weakness of each.
  - **Grandmaster:** Dragons treat you as an equal: one must hear you out before it attacks.
  - **Legendary:** Speak a dragon's true name: it must answer one question or grant one request.

### Planar Lore

*Needs Renown five. Covers: planes, rifts, outsiders*

  - **Trained:** Name the known planes and who rules them.
  - **Expert:** Recognise outsiders and the signs of a nearby rift.
  - **Master:** Name any planar being and its home plane; find a rift within a day of searching.
  - **Grandmaster:** Pass safely through a rift you find, and close it behind you.
  - **Legendary:** Know the shortest path from where you stand to any plane.

### Dreamwalking

*Needs Renown six. Covers: dreams, sleep-speech, the dreaming paths*

  - **Trained:** Remember your dreams clearly, and tell when a dream was sent by someone else.
  - **Expert:** Dream lucidly, and send a short message into a sleeping ally's dream.
  - **Master:** Once per night, enter a sleeping ally's or a willing creature's dream for one conversation.
  - **Grandmaster:** Enter an unwilling sleeper's dream (WIS save to resist) and shape what they see.
  - **Legendary:** Bring something out of a dream into the waking world, or walk the dreaming paths to wherever a sleeper lies.

### Void Lore

*Needs Renown seven. Covers: the void, its mutations, the void-touched*

  - **Trained:** Recognise void corruption and its early signs.
  - **Expert:** Slow corruption: a creature you treat gets +2 to saves against it.
  - **Master:** Read a void mutation's tier at a glance; +4 to saves against void corruption.
  - **Grandmaster:** Once per arc, draw a mutation out of someone and take it into yourself instead.
  - **Legendary:** Speak into the void and be heard: ask it one question. Once per arc.

### Truenames

*Needs Renown 8. Covers: names of power, the old paths and tiers*

  - **Trained:** Sense when a name you're told is false.
  - **Expert:** With a day's study, learn a person's real name.
  - **Master:** With a week's study, learn a creature's true name: +4 to every roll against it.
  - **Grandmaster:** Speak a true name you know: its owner makes a WIS save or loses its next turn.
  - **Legendary:** Once per arc, rename a creature or place, and change what it is.

### Godspeech

*Needs Renown 8. Covers: divine tongues, celestial and infernal rites*

  - **Trained:** Recognise divine and infernal tongues and holy signs.
  - **Expert:** Read divine texts, and speak with celestials and fiends in their own tongues.
  - **Master:** Once per arc, address a god directly; it hears you, though it may not answer.
  - **Grandmaster:** A god you address must answer, though not always truthfully.
  - **Legendary:** Once per arc, strike a pact with a god as an equal; it must keep its side.

### Starforging

*Needs Renown 10. Covers: starmetal, celestial smithing*

  - **Trained:** Recognise starmetal and celestial ore.
  - **Expert:** Smelt starmetal and shape it into simple items.
  - **Master:** Weapons you forge strike creatures of any plane at full effect, ignoring planar resistances.
  - **Grandmaster:** Forge starmetal armour that gives resistance to planar and divine damage.
  - **Legendary:** Forge an item that burns with a sun's light and can only be unmade where it was forged.

### Time Lore

*Needs Renown 11. Covers: echoes, prophecy, the weave of moments*

  - **Trained:** Sense distortions in time, and recognise echoes of the past.
  - **Expert:** Watch a place's echo: see what happened there up to a day ago.
  - **Master:** Once per session, rewind six seconds: one roll at the table is made again.
  - **Grandmaster:** Watch any past event where you stand; once per long rest, slow time to take one extra action.
  - **Legendary:** Once per arc, undo one scene entirely and play it again.

Anything not listed here, like a rare instrument or a niche trade, counts as part of the nearest skill, with the DM's approval.

# Feats

Feats are special abilities: a fighting style, a hard-won instinct, a knack for people or magic. You gain one free feat at every level X8.

## Tiers

| Tier | Open from | Power |
| **I** | Level 0 | Useful edges anyone can pick up |
| **II** | Renown 3 (level 20) | Signature tricks of a seasoned hero |
| **III** | Renown 6 (level 50) | Abilities that set legends apart |
| **IV** | Renown 8 (level 70) | Mythic: bends the rules of the world |
| **V** | Renown 11 (level 100) | Mythic: breaks them |

## Rules

  - You can take each feat once.
  - "Needs" means you must already have the named feat.
  - Uses refresh as written (per rest, per long rest, per day and so on), never faster.
  - Feats marked "caster" need a class or subclass that casts spells.
  - The italic line on each feat is flavour; the rest is the rule.

Each category below runs from Tier I up to Tier V. The higher tiers often build on a lower feat in the same table, so you can plan a path from your first feat to a mythic one.

# Traits

Traits describe what your character is, rather than what they trained to do. Your race and class traits come from their own documents. On top of those, there are three kinds of trait:

| Kind | How you get it | What it does |
| **Drawback** | Chosen at creation, or gained in play | A real weakness that gives you SP back |
| **Quirk** | Chosen at creation | A trade: one gain paired with a matching cost, for no SP |
| **Mark** | Awarded by the DM for a deed | A permanent trace of something your character survived or achieved |

## Drawbacks

  - Take at most 2 at character creation, and get back at most 3 SP from them in total.
  - A Drawback picked up during play (an eye lost to a blade) refunds its SP at your next level up.
  - If a Drawback is cured in the story, it's gone, and you don't pay the SP back.
  - The DM decides when a Drawback is forced on a character in play.

| Drawback | Refund | Effect |
| **Blind (one eye)** | 1 SP | −2 to sight-based PER rolls and to Ranged rolls beyond 30 ft. |
| **Blind (both eyes)** | 2 SP | You can't see. You sense creatures within 10 ft without sight and get advantage on hearing-based PER rolls. |
| **Deaf (one ear)** | 1 SP | −2 to hearing-based PER rolls. |
| **Deaf (both ears)** | 2 SP | You can't hear. Immune to sound-based attacks and effects. |
| **Mute** | 1 SP | You can't speak. −2 to CHA rolls that need speech; spells with a spoken part need a written or signed focus. |
| **Lame** | 1 SP | −10 ft movement and −2 to Disengage rolls. |
| **One-Handed** | 2 SP | No two-handed weapons or dual wielding; −2 to climbing and grappling. |
| **Frail** | 1 SP | −5 max HP, and you can't take Iron Skinned. |
| **Sickly** | 1 SP | Disadvantage on saves against disease; a long rest needs 10 hours. |
| **Hemophobic** | 1 SP | When you start bleeding, make a WIS save or fall unconscious for 1 round. On a pass, advantage on Disengage until combat ends. |
| **Phobia** | 1 SP | Pick a common thing (spiders, deep water, fire). Facing it, make a WIS save or be frightened for 1 minute. |
| **Spell-Shy** | 1 SP | Magical healing on you is halved. |
| **Illiterate** | 1 SP | You can't read or write, and can't take Scribing or Forgery. |
| **Honest to a Fault** | 1 SP | You can't lie well: −4 to CHA rolls to deceive. |
| **Oathbound** | 1 SP | Keep a code (never lie, never refuse a duel, never strike first). Breaking it: −2 to all rolls until you atone. |
| **Vice** | 1 SP | Drink, dice or similar. A day without it gives −2 to WIS rolls. |
| **Bad Name** | 1 SP | A town, guild or faction knows and dislikes you. −2 to CHA rolls with them; they may act against you. |
| **Hunted** | 1 SP | Someone is after you, and they turn up about once per arc. |
| **Indebted** | 1 SP | You owe someone dangerous. Once per arc they call in a job you can't refuse. |
| **Cursed** | 2 SP | A curse of the DM's choosing that flares once per session at the worst moment. |

## Quirks

Quirks cost nothing, because each comes with its own cost. Take at most 2, and a quirk can't push a stat lower than 5.

| Quirk | Gain | Cost |
| **Restless** | Improve DEX by 1 | Worsen INT by 1 |
| **Bookish** | Improve INT by 1 | Worsen STR by 1 |
| **Brute** | Improve STR by 1 | Worsen CHA by 1 |
| **Charmer** | Improve CHA by 1 | Worsen WIS by 1 |
| **Hawk-Eyed** | Improve PER by 1 | Worsen WIS by 1 |
| **Stoic** | Improve WIS by 1 | Worsen CHA by 1 |

| **Glass Cannon** | +2 damage on all attacks | −5 max HP |

| **Hasty** | +2 initiative | −2 to Dodge in the first round |
| **Nocturnal** | Advantage on PER at night | Disadvantage on PER in bright sunlight |
| **Superstitious** | +2 to saves against curses and fear | −2 to INT rolls about magic |
